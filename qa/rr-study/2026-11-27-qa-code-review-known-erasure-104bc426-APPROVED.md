## What was checked and found sound

- **The vendored path is untouched.** `ft8_extract_likelihood` is unchanged; the new `ft8_extract_likelihood_erased` sends every symbol with no erased bin through `ft8_extract_symbol` itself, so a NULL or all-zero erase row is bit-identical (and the tier-A / CQ-AP cores take an optional erase row; NULL calls the old function). Tier B (`cf_process_one`) is not touched.
- **The erased symbol reader is what the spec says.** The erase byte is indexed by the physical tone bin, which is how `kFT8_Gray_map` indexes the waterfall bins, so the erased bin of symbol value `j` is `bin = kFT8_Gray_map[j]`, consistent with the fit's tones; an erased bin's magnitude becomes the median of the non-erased bins (the average of the two middle ones for an even count); with 7 or 8 of 8 erased (`n_keep <= 1`) the three bits get likelihood 0; the three LLRs use the same `max4` expressions as the ordinary reader.
- **The mask layout and the overlap rule are right.** The mask index `blk * block_stride + (ts * freq_osr + fs) * num_bins + bin` is the waterfall's own (as `ftz_probe_footprint_power` and `get_cand_mag`); a known symbol marks the time cells `|c - x| <= time_osr - 1` (every cell whose window overlaps the symbol) and tone index `+-FTZ_ERASE_FREQ_HALFWIDTH` oversampled bins (1: 3.125 Hz); counts saturate at 65,535; every write is bounds-checked against the waterfall and the mask length; non-finite and absurd positions are not placed.
- **Self exemption** within `FTZ_ERASE_SELF_HZ` / `FTZ_ERASE_SELF_S` (3.2 Hz, 0.16 s) is done by decrementing the per-cell count for the known results that cover the candidate, up to 16 such results; the rows are built on the main thread before the jobs and the workers only read them.
- **Scope is exactly the spec's:** `if (round > 0)` only (round 0 never builds a mask); `cyc` is zeroed per round (`memset`), so `cyc.erase` is NULL unless this round built rows (no stale rows from an earlier round); the known results are the cycle's `results[]` at SNR >= -10 dB with their tones (`res_tones`, filled where each result is stored, including replaced slots) in rounds 1 to 3, and in the XC round the handed-in known results plus the detections at >= -10 dB; the XC round's own `results[]` starts empty, so only the handed-in ones count. Search, subtraction, tier B, accept rules, batches and the DR-3 rule are unchanged.
- **Design decisions are sound and reported:** `ftz_params_t` version 7 with `out_tones`; `ftz_xc_t` gains the known arrays and `det_snr`; `LeanCycleParams.WantTones` is set only when the stage is on; `ftz_xc_round` refuses a malformed known array with `FTZ_ERR_ARG`; the counters (`n_a_cands`, `n_a_erased`) are in the round stats and the log line, as asked.
- **Tests:** (a) the mask is compared with an independent brute-force expectation from the spec's definition, with the exact -10 dB boundary (in), -11 (nothing), no known result, and cells outside the waterfall or absurd positions; (c) a NULL or far-away known result gives bit-identical likelihoods, a candidate on the known result is not erased on itself and still decodes, round-0 results and the round-0 CAND rows' numeric columns are the same with the rounds on and off on every committed `synth-qso-*.wav`, and the XC round with known results is the same bytes at 1, 2 and 16 threads and reports the counters. No delays; synthetic `Q`-prefix content only.
- **Documents:** FR-108 amended (the residual-round sentence, 20260083 in the shim list, the XC round's known results, the counters), changelog 1.81, OpenSpec section 15 and a delta with three scenarios; no `VERSION` bump required.

## The risk the replay settles (not a defect)

The Developer's test (b) could not show the specified effect (a weak signal failing tier A without the mask and decoding with it) on any geometry the helpers can build: scanning spacings of 6.25 to 31 Hz, three time offsets and leftovers of -3 to +15 dB, the weak signal fails tier A with and without the mask. What the mask does change is the weak signal's raw bit errors against the truth: **478 down to 437 of 8 x 174 over 8 cases**, lower in 6 of 8, 1 to 2 bits worse in two, and **14 to 18 errors from none when the weak signal is as strong as the known one or stronger**. The Developer's explanation: a Hann window leaks about -6 dB into the neighbour bins 6.25 Hz away, which the specified width (+-1 oversampled bin, 3.125 Hz) does not cover; widths of 2 and 3 did not rescue it in a scratch trial. So the change may move K2 only a little; the replay decides, and the Architect should read this note with the replay (the width is the named constant `FTZ_ERASE_FREQ_HALFWIDTH`).

## Minor notes (none blocks)

1. **Test (b) is weak by necessity:** it asserts that the plain reading fails tier A, that the masked reading has fewer bit errors over the cases (and at most 3 more in any one), and nothing about a decode. It is honest about it; the replay is the real test.
2. **Round 0 is not byte-compared against the 20260082 binary** (the test compares round 0 with the rounds on and off in the same build): the replay's free check (the round-0 CAND rows' numeric columns identical to the `xn_*` runs in every cycle) is the comparison across builds.
3. **The per-round log group prints `aCands` / `aErased` only when the round had tier-A candidates** (an existing test pins the exact text of the group): a reader must treat absent fields as 0. QA's analysis does; QA's harness regex for the `xcRound[...]` group was updated for the two new fields (named groups; without it its `xr_*` columns would have read zero silently).
4. **`DecodeLeanXcRound` gains a defaulted `known` parameter** on the interface (and the adapter); implementations that override it must follow; the managed test double was adapted, no test body edited.
5. The earlier reviews' minor notes on `65bca805` stand (the native duplicate rule sees only the call's own results; a tone above 7 reported as `FTZ_ERR_NOMEM`; a pinned probe handle can leak on an exception).
