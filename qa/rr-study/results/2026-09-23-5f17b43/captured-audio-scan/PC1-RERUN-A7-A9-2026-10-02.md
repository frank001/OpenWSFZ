**Mechanism (diagnosed on the three missed copies, descriptive, no redraw):** the miss is **not** a weak response; it is that the injected copy **becomes `lag_ambiguous`**, so `drift_ppm` is NaN and cannot flag. Slot `S1_p007_t000`, injected +ppm resample: 10 ppm reads −9.9 ppm (correct, sign aside); 20, 40, 80, 160 ppm all read NaN. `S4_p003_t001`: 10 → −9.2, 20 → −18.3, 40, 80, 160 → NaN. The A3 detector (second distinct correlation peak ≥ 0.9 × best, "distinct" = more than 6 samples from the best) is evaluated **on the captured slot**: a drift of ~30 ppm or more moves the lag by more than 6 samples across the slot, which smears the correlation peak into shoulders beyond ±6 samples, and the detector reads the smear as an ambiguous reference. The same happens to the 5-sample insertion of the `hiccup` at 2× (step_db_max 31 dB there, drift NaN). **This is an HK-026 defect in the instrument: a timing fault large enough to matter converts itself into "ambiguous lag", which switches the timing family off.** It did not stop the 12 real 108 to 144 sample lag steps on 09-23 (a step produces two clean peaks of unequal weight, and those slots were not classed ambiguous), but a smooth drift of > ~30 ppm, or a hiccup splitting a slot near its middle, can hide.

**What I propose, and do not apply (it changes `scan_core.py`, so it needs your amendment and a new freeze):** decide ambiguity from the **reference alone**, not from the capture: A3′ = the reference's own autocorrelation (against itself, lags ±3.5 s, excluding ±6 samples) has a second peak ≥ 0.9 × its zero-lag peak. That is a property of the signal that was played (steady carriers, repeated structure), independent of any fault in the chain, and for clean slots it should reproduce the existing 68 ambiguous slots on 09-23 (to be checked).
