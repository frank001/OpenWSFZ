## 0. Why (one paragraph)

On the night `20261110_1817`, 22.9 % of the WSJT-X rows we missed (520 rows, 3.72 pp of the reference) are exact repeats of a message we had decoded 30 to 90 s earlier within 10 Hz (QA's XC-PRIOR). A test that knows the **whole message** fixes all 79 tones and needs no LDPC: summing the known tone's energy over the 79 symbols, in a ±3.125 Hz × ±0.12 s window around the prior's position **on the fallback's residual buffer**, detected 51 % of them at a measured false rate of 4.3 × 10⁻⁴ per test (XC-TEMPLATE, a desk study: G = 1.91 pp). This task builds that stage as **batch 6**, default OFF, with a **shadow null** (the same test on the opposite-parity priors, counted and never published) so the arms can measure the false rate instead of inheriting it. 🔴 **No chaining:** a message published by this stage never becomes a prior.

| **R5 Detector** | The XC-TEMPLATE statistic. One downconvert at the prior's frequency, the other 12 frequencies by **shifting the decimated baseband** (`bb[n] * exp(-j 2π δ n / 2000)`): QA checked this equals a direct downconvert to 6 × 10⁻⁴ of the maximum at 10 Hz. **Managed C# only**; no native change (see the top). `calibration` = **0.48 s** (the frozen start-minus-dt between WSJT-X's dt and the product's start convention; name it as a constant with that origin). **Fixtures (below) pin the numbers.** |
