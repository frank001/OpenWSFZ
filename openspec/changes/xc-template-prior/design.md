## Decisions

**D1. C#-only, behind a setting, default OFF.** OFF is the code as it was on every path. The guard is one local, `bool xcOn = cohOn && _crossCyclePrior` (`Ft8Decoder.DecodeCoreInnerAsync`), read once per cycle. Everything that writes the store, allocates the per-cycle published-text set, opens a store cycle, runs the stage or writes the line is behind it (`xcPublished` is null while OFF, and `BeginCycle` is not called, so `Record` has no cycle to write into: two independent guards). `cohOn` already requires a staged cycle with all three callbacks, so the early decode, the single-batch path and `DecodeTwoStageAsync` never take part (R2).

**D2. The detector is a port, with two speed-ups that change no number.** The reference is `xt_lib.search` on `coherent_extract.downconvert_decimate` / `correlate_symbols`. (1) The DFT of a symbol's 320 baseband samples is ROLLED across the 25 start times (they are 20 samples apart): the update subtracts 20 samples, adds 20 and rotates by `exp(+j 2 pi b 20 / 320)`, instead of a fresh 320 x 8 product. A symbol whose chunks leave the buffer at any start time, or a start time that is not exactly 20 samples from the previous, is computed directly with zero padding. (2) The mixer and the frequency shift use a phasor recurrence re-seeded from an exact `sin`/`cos` every 256 samples. The fixtures pin the result: S_E* within 1e-3 relative and the argmax equal on seven cases, the 61 taps, four baseband checkpoints and the 79 x 8 powers at the true position; a test compares the rolled grid with a direct computation to 1e-9. Search order and the strict `>` are the reference's, so ties resolve alike.

**D3. Cost.** About 9 to 15 ms per template single-threaded on the development machine (the desk estimate was 30 to 50 ms); 49 templates take about 0.4 s on one worker and about 60 ms on 14. The stage runs on up to `EffectiveSubtractionThreads` workers that claim templates in index (nearest-first) order.

**D4. The store.** Keyed by the cycle's 15 s SLOT (the start rounded to the nearest 15 s), so the framing jitter of the live `CycleFramer` and the exact stamps Replay81 passes land on the same slot. Live priors are those at slot - 2, - 4, - 6 (30, 60, 90 s) and shadow priors at slot - 1, - 3, - 5; a missing cycle therefore leaves a hole and never shifts an age. Entries older than 6 slots, or from the future (a clock that jumped back), are dropped at `BeginCycle`; a band change (a non-equal band string, null on both sides counting as equal) clears it. What is stored is the native result's frequency and dt (not the rounded published values), so the window is centred on the real position. Every member takes one lock: the decoder is called once per cycle under the daemon's decode gate, so there is no contention, and the lock is for safety only. Worker threads never touch the store: they read an immutable template list.

**D5. No chaining.** Only `MapNative` calls with an `xcBatch` of 1 to 5 record. The batch-6 mapping passes none, so a published template is never a prior. A test asserts the store count after a batch-6 publish and the age at which the next cycle sees the message.

**D6. Where batch 6 sits.** After the late round (batch 5), or after the fallback's batch when the late round is off, at the three places the staged path ends (nothing decoded, Normal depth with the fallback, Deep). The late-round-off Normal-depth path that returns batch 2 to the caller does not run the stage (it would publish batch 6 before batch 2). The buffer is `CohFallbackAudioSelector.Select(...)` exactly as the late round takes it (R3 if the third pass ran, else R2); none is a counted `skipped-no-residual`. The buffer is read only. The native encoder is entered only on the calling thread, one text at a time.

**D7. R7 details.** Frequency: the argmax rounded with banker's rounding to integer Hz, the same rounding `CohFallbackAudioSelector.PublishedFreqHz` applies. dt: the argmax's dt, which is in the convention of the prior it was found around (the native result's dt that batches 1 to 5 publish); `MapNative` rounds it to 0.1 s as for every batch. SNR: `CrossCycleTemplateDetector.CoherentSnrDb`, the managed form of the native `cf_snr_db` that every fallback row's SNR comes from (`coherent_fallback.c`: the selected tone's mean power over the others', `(sel - oth) / oth`, times 6.25 / 2500, floored at -30 dB, `lround`), evaluated at the argmax with a direct downconvert. Same per-cycle text de-duplication and plausibility filter as every batch.

**D8. Consumers of a sixth batch.** `DecodePump`, `PublishSecondAsync`, the answerer and caller services (`BatchNumber > 1` is a supplementary batch), `ALL.TXT`, the panel and external reporting take the batch number generically: none assumes at most 5. Two observations are NOT changed here (spec section 3 forbids touching them) and go to QA: (a) the QSO queues' capacity (`LateBatch.BatchesPerCycleFallbackOn` = 4, so 10) was already one batch short of batch 5; batch 6 adds one more under a blocked QSO service, DropOldest; (b) a batch-6 row is uploaded to PSK Reporter like any other decode (there is no template tag in the upload filter, unlike `apKind` = `cq`). Both matter only with the setting ON and a finite T_E.

**D9. The shadow.** Runs after the live batch has been handed off, so it can never delay a publish; same detector, window, T_E and exclusions on the priors at ages 1, 3, 5, against the same buffer; counted and logged, never published, never stored. It needs `crossCyclePrior` on: with the main flag off it does nothing.

**D10. The sink (R13).** `ICrossCyclePriorProbe` (internal; reachable by Replay81 through `InternalsVisibleTo("Ft8.FitProbe")`), property `Ft8Decoder.CrossCyclePriorProbe`, null by default. Called after each parallel run, on the decoding thread, in test order, once per TESTED template (never for an abandoned one) with a `CrossCycleTestRecord` of numbers only (kind, S_E*, age in cycles, argmax offsets from the prior in Hz and s, cycle stamp); a test asserts the record has no string, array or payload member. A sink that throws is logged and ignored.

**D11. Budget.** The stage stops STARTING templates at `SubtractionCycleBudget - CoherentFallbackPublishReserve` (13 s - 250 ms) on the decode's own clock; the started ones finish, so a template is never cut short and a detection finished before the deadline publishes. Abandoned templates are counted (`abandoned`, `shadowAbandoned`).

## Risks

- T_E is a placeholder: with the shipped value nothing is detected (by design). The real value comes from QA's TRAIN arm and must be put in before any merge.
- A hashed message (`<...>`) cannot be re-encoded and is excluded and counted; 14.4 % of the E1 rows are of that kind.
- The false rate at T_E is known to a factor of 2 to 3 (split-half): the shadow null exists for that.
- The window is centred on the stored native position, not on WSJT-X's: the calibration (0.48 s) is the frozen start-minus-dt of TA-CHECK and is a named constant with that origin.
